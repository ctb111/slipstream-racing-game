PBR material sets used by scripts/world/materials.gd (Mats)
============================================================
All sets are CC0 1.0 (public domain) - no attribution required, credited anyway.
Files per set: <set>_diff.jpg (albedo), <set>_nor_gl.jpg (OpenGL normal),
<set>_arm.jpg (R = ambient occlusion, G = roughness, B = metalness), 1024 px.
For ambientCG sets the ARM map was packed locally from the AO / Roughness /
Metalness maps (missing channels: AO = 1, metal = 0).
