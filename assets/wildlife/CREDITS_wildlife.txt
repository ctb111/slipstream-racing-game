Wildlife kit models (assets/wildlife/models, used by scripts/world/wildlife.gd)
=============================================================================
All from Sketchfab, obtained through the Objaverse dataset (Allen Institute for
AI, https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse; it redistributes the
creators' own licences). Re-posed / joined / scaled / decimated and textures
shrunk by assets/wildlife/bake/bake_animals.py (Blender, headless); the deer is
untextured in the source and is painted in shaders/wildlife.gdshader.
Animation is procedural (vertex shader), not from the sources.
