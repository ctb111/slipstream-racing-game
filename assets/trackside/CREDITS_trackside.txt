Trackside atlases (assets/trackside/trackside_paint.png, trackside_signs.png)
  Original artwork of this project, generated by tools/make_trackside_atlas.py
  (graffiti pieces, tags, throw-ups, stencils, painted fan messages, banners,
  warning signs, timing board, flags). All brands, teams and names are invented.
  No third-party images are used.
