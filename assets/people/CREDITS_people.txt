People / crowd figures — sources and licences
=============================================

All human figures (spectators, grandstand crowd, photographers, pit crew,
bystanders, flag marshals) are generated from MakeHuman assets, which are
released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). No attribution is legally
required; we credit the authors anyway.

Base humans, skins, eyes, eyebrows, hair, clothes and shoes
  "makehuman_system_assets" asset pack, MakeHuman Community — CC0 1.0
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
  (download: https://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip)
  Copyright holders at the time of the CC0 release (Sept 2020): Data Collection AB,
  Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier. Assets used:
    base mesh + macro targets (gender, age, muscle, weight, height, proportions)
    skins:   middleage_caucasian_male, young_caucasian_male, young_caucasian_male2,
             young_caucasian_female, middleage_caucasian_female
    clothes: male_casualsuit01, male_casualsuit05, male_worksuit01,
             female_casualsuit01, female_elegantsuit01
    shoes:   shoes01, shoes02, shoes03, shoes05, shoes06
    hair:    short01, short02, short04, ponytail01, bob02
    eyebrows: eyebrow001, eyebrow002, eyebrow010; eyes: low-poly

Tooling (not redistributed, used to build the assets)
  MPFB 2.0.17 (MakeHuman plugin for Blender) — GPL-3.0-or-later, Joel Palmius et al.
  https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/mpfb/  (its output is not covered by the GPL;
  the character data comes from the CC0 assets above)
  Blender 5.2 — GPL; Mixamo-compatible skeleton from MPFB's built-in "mixamo" rig.

Files
  assets/people/src/*_{hi,mid,lo}.glb   rigged humans exported by bake/make_humans.py
                                        (Blender Decimate for the mid/lo LODs; .gdignore'd)
  assets/people/meshes/*.res            posed static meshes baked by bake/bake_people.gd
  assets/people/people_atlas.png        texture atlas packed from the CC0 textures

Rebuild
  1. Blender 5.2 with MPFB installed into a private profile, and the CC0 system asset
     pack unzipped into MPFB's user data directory:
       BLENDER_USER_RESOURCES=<profile> Blender -b --command extension install-file -r user_default -e add-on-mpfb-v2.0.17.zip
       unzip makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip -d <profile>/extensions/.user/user_default/mpfb/data
  2. BLENDER_USER_RESOURCES=<profile> Blender -b --python assets/people/bake/make_humans.py -- <out dir>
     then copy <out>/*_hi.glb (textures resized to 1024 with
     `npx @gltf-transform/cli resize --width 1024 --height 1024`) and *_mid/_lo.glb into assets/people/src.
  3. godot --headless --path . --script res://assets/people/bake/bake_people.gd
  4. godot --headless --path . --import
  Preview: tools/bgrun.sh res://assets/people/bake/preview.tscn -- --out=<png> [--lod=mid|lo]
           [--crowd=80 --nolineup] [--stand=full --nolineup --cam=x,y,z,tx,ty,tz]
